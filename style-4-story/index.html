私たちのこと · Who we are

The little tea-dog
of Manchester.

OHAYO TEA is a Shiba-Inu-themed tea house in the heart of Manchester — proud to be the first tea company in the city to brew fresh fruit tea into our Fresh Fruit Tea series.

We use quality ingredients and a professional handcrafted technique, so every cup is a little piece of art. Nothing rushed, nothing powdered where it matters.

「飲めばわかる。」— Drink it, don't ask why.
