Who are we

A tea-dog
with attitude

OHAYO TEA is a Shiba-Inu-themed tea house in the heart of Manchester — the first tea company in the city to brew fresh fruit tea into our Fresh Fruit Tea series.

Quality ingredients. Professional handcrafted technique. Every single cup a piece of art. No powders where it matters, no compromises. Just ohayo and go.
