Who are we

The little tea-dog of Manchester

OHAYO TEA is a Shiba Inu-themed tea house in the heart of Manchester. We're proud to be the first tea company in Manchester to bring freshly brewed fruit tea into our Fresh Fruit Tea series.

We use quality ingredients and professional handcrafted technique, so every cup of tea is a unique piece of art — mindful, generous and unmistakably ours.
