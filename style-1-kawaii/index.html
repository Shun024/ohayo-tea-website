Who are we?

A little tea-dog with big flavour

OHAYO TEA is a Shiba Inu-themed tea house in the heart of Manchester. We're proud to be the first tea company in Manchester to bring freshly brewed fruit tea into our Fresh Fruit Tea series — no shortcuts, no powders where it counts.

Every cup is made with quality ingredients and professional handcrafted technique, so each drink is a little piece of art. That's the OHAYO promise — drink it, don't ask why!
